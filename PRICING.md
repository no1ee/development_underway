## Summary & Recommendation

**Recommended Launch Strategy:**

1. **Start with 3 tiers**: Community (free), Professional ($299), Business ($999)
2. **Offer 14-day free trial** on paid tiers
3. **Launch with early bird discount** (40% off first year)
4. **Target initial 100 customers** with lifetime discounts
5. **Add Enterprise tier** after 50 paid customers (validate demand)
6. **Introduce industry packages** in Year 2 based on usage data

**Key Pricing Principles:**
- Value-based: Price based on customer revenue protection, not cost
- Land and expand: Start with Professional, upsell to Business
- Annual contracts: Push annual with 17% discount to reduce churn
- Usage tiers: Align pricing with customer scale (MAU limits)
- Premium positioning: Not the cheapest, but the most comprehensive

**Expected Metrics:**
- Free-to-paid conversion: 3-5%
- Trial-to-paid conversion: 25-30%
- Annual subscription rate: 60%+
- Churn: <5% monthly (SaaS standard)
- CAC payback: 6-12 months
- LTV:CAC ratio: 3:1 minimum

This pricing strategy balances accessibility for smaller developers while capturing significant value from larger enterprises who derive the most benefit from the protection.
